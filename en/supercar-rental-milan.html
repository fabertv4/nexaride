FAQ

Which cars can I rent?+
Ferrari, Lamborghini, Porsche and other sports cars through vetted partner rental firms. Real availability depends on your dates: we confirm it on WhatsApp.
How do deposit and insurance work?+
Each rental firm applies its own deposit and excess. The exact terms are sent to you in writing before you confirm: no surprises at pickup.
Which documents do I need?+
A valid driving licence and ID; for non-EU licences rental firms usually require an International Driving Permit. Minimum age and licence seniority vary by model.
Do you deliver to hotels or the airport?+
Yes: most partners deliver and collect wherever you prefer, in Milan and the surrounding area.
Can I have it with a chauffeur?+
Yes, on request we also arrange the chauffeured option: handy for events, shoots, or an evening when you just want to enjoy the car.
Can I pay in crypto?+
Yes, on request: we accept Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH) and USDT/USDC stablecoins, alongside card and cash. Agree your quote on WhatsApp as usual and you'll receive a payment link; the amount stays the euro price agreed.
