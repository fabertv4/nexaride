Bergamo Airport ⇄ Milan:
bus, taxi or private driver?

Italy's third-busiest airport still has no train: it arrives in December 2026. Today the backbone of the route is the bus to Milano Centrale — 50-55 minutes for 8-10 €, from 3 am to past midnight — and for solo travellers it works very well. Taxis have a fixed fare (128 €), and a pre-booked car wins on door-to-door, groups and the night windows. Verified timetables and tariffs, both directions.

The train that is not there (yet)

It surprises everyone who knows Malpensa: Orio al Serio, Italy's third-busiest airport, has no rail link today.

A new 5.3 km line between Bergamo station and the airport is under construction, with a station connected to the arrivals hall by a pedestrian tunnel. Opening is planned for December 2026: once live, expect ~58 minutes from Milano Centrale and 154 trains a day. Until then, rail means going via Bergamo city: a regional train to Bergamo FS, then the ATB Linea 1 city bus to the terminal (~15 minutes, every ~20, ticket 2.40-3.00 € depending on where you buy). Doable, but with a change and bags in hand: it mainly makes sense if Bergamo is on your way anyway.

We will update this page when the station opens.

The bus: the backbone of the route

And it deserves saying: for solo travellers it works very well.

Direct buses (Terravision, Orio Shuttle, Flibco) link the airport to Milano Centrale in ~50-55 minutes for 8-10 € one-way (online promos from 5 €). Departures average every 20 minutes and the service window is huge — first runs around 3 am, last after midnight — because the timetable is built around the flights.

The limits are two, and they are structural: you depart and arrive only at Milano Centrale (the last mile to your hotel or apartment is on you, bags included), and at peak times a full bus means waiting for the next one. By car the route is 45-50 km, 50-60 minutes depending on traffic.

When a pre-booked car is the right tool

An NCC does not compete with the bus on a solo traveller's price — it competes where the bus stops:

  • 🏨 Door-to-door. The bus stops only at Centrale: if your hotel is near the Duomo, in Navigli or outside the centre, the last mile with suitcases is yours. A pre-booked car arrives at the address.
  • 🧳 Groups and families. With 3-4 people and luggage, the per-car quote compares with the fixed taxi (128 €), not with a single bus ticket: a 5+ seat van booked with certainty, kids and bags with no transfers.
  • 🌙 Night and delays. In the buses' uncovered window (~00:10-3:10 am) or after a delayed flight, flight monitoring adjusts the pickup for free and the first hour of waiting is included: the car is there when you actually land.

The 3 Bergamo-route mistakes

The ones that stretch a low-cost traveller's night.

  • 🚆 Looking for the airport train. It does not exist yet: until December 2026 the rails stop at Bergamo city. Arriving at Centrale expecting a platform for Orio costs you the flight or the wallet.
  • 🗺️ Reading "Milan" on the ticket and nothing else. The airport is in Bergamo, 45-50 km from central Milan: the last leg needs a plan, not an improvisation at arrivals with your bags.
  • 🌙 Improvising after midnight with a family. The last bus is ~00:10 and service resumes at 3 am: with children and luggage, a delayed evening flight turns the wait into a long night. The hard cases are solved by booking ahead.

Frequently asked questions

How much is the bus from Bergamo Airport to Milan?+
Direct buses to Milano Centrale (Terravision, Orio Shuttle, Flibco) cost roughly 8-10 € one-way, with online promos from 5 €. The ride takes about 50-55 minutes and departures average every 20 minutes; first runs leave around 3 am and the last after midnight, because the timetable follows the flights.
Is there a train from Bergamo Airport to Milan?+
Not yet: the airport has no direct rail link today. A new 5.3 km line with a station connected to arrivals by a pedestrian tunnel is under construction, planned to open in December 2026 (~58 minutes from Milano Centrale once live). Meanwhile the rail alternative is indirect: a regional train to Bergamo plus the ATB city bus Linea 1 to the airport (~15 minutes, every ~20).
How much is a taxi from Bergamo Airport to Milan?+
The fare is fixed: 128 € per car to Milan city centre, set by the Comune di Milano, the same day and night. Rides into Bergamo city run on the meter instead.
I land after midnight - how do I get to Milan?+
Buses cover most of the night: last runs around 00:10 and service resumes around 3:10 am. In the uncovered window - or with children and luggage after a delayed flight - the robust option is a pre-booked car with flight monitoring: the pickup adjusts to the real landing time and the first hour of waiting is included.
Group of 4 with luggage: bus or private car?+
On pure price the bus still wins (4 tickets ≈ 32-40 €), and that deserves saying. The private car wins on everything else: door-to-door to your hotel or apartment (the bus stops ONLY at Milano Centrale), no waiting or transfers with bags, a per-car quote agreed in advance on WhatsApp and a 5+ seat van booked with certainty.
If my flight into Bergamo lands late, does the transfer wait?+
Yes: give us the flight number and the pickup adjusts to the real landing time at no extra cost - flight delays never count against you. From the actual landing, the first hour of waiting is included in the price.
