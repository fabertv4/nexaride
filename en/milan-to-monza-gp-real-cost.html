The Real Cost of Getting From Milan
to the 2026 Italian Grand Prix

Trains, taxis, apps and private chauffeurs on race weekend (September 4-6, 2026): what actually works, what it costs, and the mistakes that make international visitors overspend. Research by NexaRide, a Milan chauffeur (NCC) operator, based on our real booking data and our drivers' first-hand experience at the Autodromo.

Data: NexaRide booking logs (August 2026) · official Trenord and Comune di Milano tariffs · driver operations at past editions. No provider rankings.

Real timing: getting in is easy, getting out is not

The geography of the problem: Monza is 20 km from Milan, but on race day the rules change.

On the way in, on race morning, traffic towards the park is still manageable: our drivers recommend leaving Milan between 7:00 and 7:30 am. Leave later and you meet growing queues at the approaches and the first filters.

The real bottleneck is after the race: the roads around the park are closed in alternating phases to let the crowds flow out, and journey times become unpredictable even for drivers who know the area well.

The most delicate case is combining the race with a flight: Malpensa-Monza (in either direction) on race day should always be treated as at risk. Heavy traffic plus alternating closures mean the normal journey time tells you nothing: for an evening flight after the race, allow a much larger buffer than usual.

Where cars can actually take you

Licensed chauffeur cars (NCC) and taxis can generally get through to the closest permitted drop-off point to the gates: that is simply how access is organised on race day.

The detail almost no visitor knows: on race day many chauffeurs work by the hour and stay "inside" all day. The reason is practical: from some zones, once you leave, you cannot get back in. That is why the typical GP-weekend format is not a simple A-to-B transfer but a car and driver staying on site at your disposal.

For the dedicated event service, see the Monza Grand Prix transfer page.

The 3 mistakes that cost visitors real money

The ones we see repeated every edition, especially by international visitors.

  • 🕐 Booking late. Demand runs at ~10× normal and fleets fill up a month out. Book close to the date and you are choosing from the leftovers, at leftover prices.
  • 📍 Insisting on the "most convenient" pickup point. The spot that looks best on the map is often inside a closure or unreachable during the outflow. The meeting point your driver proposes, perhaps 300 metres away, beats an hour stuck in a queue.
  • 📱 Expecting the big apps to cover extreme demand. Everyone requests a car at the same moment: supply cannot keep up by definition, and surge pricing stacks on top. The apps work brilliantly in Milan on a Tuesday evening; on GP Sunday they do not.

Frequently asked questions

How much is the train from Milan to the Monza GP?+
For race weekend Trenord sells a 13 € day-return ticket valid from any station in Lombardy, plus the shuttle from Monza station to the circuit (6 € per day). Alternatively, the S7 line from Milano Porta Garibaldi stops at Biassono-Lesmo Parco, a short walk from the Lesmo-side entrance.
Is there a fixed taxi fare from Milan to Monza?+
No: Milan-Monza runs on the meter. The fixed fares set by the Comune di Milano only exist on certain airport routes (for example Malpensa-Milan city centre at 114 €). On GP weekend, taxi availability at peak times is very limited.
How far in advance should I book a private transfer for the GP?+
Weeks, not days. For the 2026 edition, requests reached roughly 10 times those of a normal weekend and our fleet was fully booked by early August, a month before the race - a situation most Milan chauffeur operators we compare notes with reported as well.
What time should I leave Milan on race day?+
Our drivers recommend leaving between 7:00 and 7:30 am. The morning run in is the calm part of the day; the real bottleneck is the exit after the chequered flag, when the roads around the park are closed in alternating phases to let the crowds flow out.
Can a private car drop me at the circuit gates?+
Licensed chauffeur cars (NCC) and taxis can generally reach the closest permitted drop-off point to the gates. On race day many chauffeurs work by the hour and stay inside the area all day: from some zones, once you leave, you cannot get back in.
Do Uber-style apps work after the race?+
It is the worst-case scenario for the apps: tens of thousands of people request a car at the same moment, supply cannot cover the extreme demand and surge pricing kicks in. A seat on the train, or a car booked in advance with an agreed meeting point, beats waiting on a multiplier.

Methodology and sources

This article is part of the NexaRide research series on transport and mobility in Milan.

Internal data: booking requests received (website and WhatsApp) for the September 4-6, 2026 weekend, compared with September weekends without major events; NexaRide fleet capacity and saturation; our drivers' operational experience at past editions of the GP.

Public sources: Trenord (special GP ticket and Autodromo shuttle), taxi tariffs set by the Comune di Milano.
