Milan to The Mall Florence:
Train + Shuttle, Private Car or a Stopover

The luxury maisons' outlet is not in Florence: it is in Leccio, 30 km further south-east - and no public transport runs there direct from Milan. The Frecciarossa takes ~1h50 to Firenze S.M.N., then you need an hour-long bus: door to door, the fast train does not beat the road. The problem is not Florence: it is the last mile

The Milan-Florence high-speed line is excellent. But it drops you at Firenze S.M.N., and The Mall is 30 km further out.

From there, the only dedicated public link is Busitalia's The Mall by Bus: it leaves from Piazzale Montelungo (about ten minutes' walk from the station), the ride takes about one hour and the ticket costs €9.50 one way or €18 return. Departures cover the day (morning outbound runs, evening returns in the summer period), but they are fixed times: the last useful bus decides when your shopping ends.

The door-to-door maths from Milan: ~1h50 of train + the walk + the wait + ~1h of bus = ~3h-3h30. Which is what the car takes. Starting from Florence, the shuttle is a great deal; starting from Milan, the fast train's advantage evaporates in the last mile - and on the way back you travel with your shopping bags in hand.

Before you go: the alternatives one hour from Milan

Honestly: if the goal is "an outlet day starting from Milan", you do not need to cross half of Italy. Serravalle Designer Outlet is about one hour away, and so is FoxTown Mendrisio (across the Swiss border). And on the Riviera corridor there is also The Mall Sanremo, the Ligurian sibling of the same group.

The Mall Florence is worth the journey for one precise reason: the mono-brand outlets of the great maisons (Gucci, Prada, Bottega Veneta, Burberry and the like) in a format the north does not have. If that is what you are after, the 3.5 hours make sense; if not, Serravalle wins on logistics.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take from Milan to The Mall Florence by car?+
About 330 km: the A1 motorway to the Incisa-Reggello exit, then some ten minutes to Leccio. In normal conditions allow ~3h15-3h45 depending on A1 traffic. These are prudent road estimates: the traffic of the day prevails.
How do I get to The Mall from Milan by public transport?+
There is no direct connection. The real route is a Frecciarossa or Italo train from Milano Centrale to Firenze S.M.N. (~1h50), then the official "The Mall by Bus" shuttle by Busitalia from Piazzale Montelungo (about ten minutes' walk from the station): ~1 hour ride, €9.50 one way or €18 return. Door to door, with the change and waits, ~3h-3h30.
What are The Mall Florence opening hours?+
The outlet is open every day from 10 am to 8 pm, at Via Europa 8, Leccio Reggello (FI). For holidays and exceptions, the official The Mall website prevails.
From Milan, is The Mall or Serravalle the better choice?+
It depends on what you are after. Serravalle Designer Outlet is about one hour from Milan and is the rational pick for a general shopping day; The Mall is worth the 3.5-hour journey if you want the mono-brand outlets of the big maisons (Gucci, Prada, Bottega Veneta, Burberry and the like). They are two different products, not two versions of the same outlet.
How does a private Milan-The Mall transfer with waiting work?+
It is a full-day service: a dedicated car takes you there, the driver waits while you shop and drives you back to Milan; the waiting time is agreed when you ask for the quote. Your purchases travel in the car, not in your hands on a bus. Quotes are quick on WhatsApp.
Can I stop at The Mall during a transfer from Milan to Florence or Tuscany?+
Yes - and it is often the most efficient way: Leccio sits ~30 km from Florence right on the A1 corridor, so the shopping stop fits into the journey without major detours. Agree on it in advance together with the rest of the transfer.
