FAQ

Which destinations can you reach by helicopter?+
The classics: Lake Como, St. Moritz and the Engadin, Portofino and the Riviera. Other routes are possible on request, depending on helipads and permits.
Does NexaRide operate the flights directly?+
No: flights are operated by certified helicopter companies, sourced through partners. NexaRide arranges the request and the ground transfers.
What if the weather grounds the flight?+
That's the advantage of messaging us: if the flight can't operate, the same request becomes a premium chauffeured transfer by road, without starting over.
How much does it cost?+
It depends on the route, date and aircraft. Private quote on WhatsApp with departure, destination, date and passengers.
Where do you take off from?+
From helipads and airfields in the Milan area, confirmed by the partner depending on the chosen route.
Can I pay in crypto?+
Yes, on request: we accept Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH) and USDT/USDC stablecoins, alongside card and cash. Agree your quote on WhatsApp as usual and you'll receive a payment link; the amount stays the euro price agreed.
Can I combine the helicopter with a chauffeured car in one itinerary?+
Yes — that is the advantage of arranging the flight through a chauffeur service: NexaRide coordinates in a single quote the car to the helipad and a car waiting at landing, in Milan or at your destination. One WhatsApp contact for the flight and all ground transfers.
What do people charter helicopters from Milan for?+
Typical requests: fast transfers to the lakes and the Alps (Como, St. Moritz, Portofino), scenic flights, weddings and events, business trips on tight schedules. Flights are operated by AOC-certified operators; NexaRide arranges the full logistics, from take-off to ground transfers.
