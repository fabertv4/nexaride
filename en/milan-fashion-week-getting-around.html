The bottleneck: the hours before each show

Unlike the GP, the problem is not one big exodus: it is a peak that repeats several times a day, for seven days.

The critical time is before each show, when the movements of buyers, press and guests stack on top of the ordinary traffic of a city that keeps working. The result: urban hops that normally take 15 minutes can take far longer exactly when your margin is zero, because the show calendar waits for no one.

Our drivers' rule is simple: move with a generous time buffer. Plan your trips on "normal day" timings and you arrive late.

Arriving: Linate and Malpensa

Fashion Week brings clients to Milan from all over the world, mostly via Linate and Malpensa, with arrivals concentrated in the days immediately before the calendar opens.

If you land with an appointment already in your diary, the same logic as in-city moves applies: secure the car beforehand, do not go looking for one on arrival.

The mistakes that cost time (and appointments)

The ones we see repeated every edition.

  • 📱 Expecting app cars to be instantly available. The classic mistake: at Fashion Week peak times immediate availability is not guaranteed, and a delay to a show cannot be recovered.
  • 🕐 Planning trips on "normal day" timings. Fashion traffic stacks on ordinary congestion: without a generous pre-show buffer, you arrive late.
  • 📅 Booking last-minute. With requests at ~10× normal, anyone with fixed appointments who has not secured their own chauffeur in time is left to the mercy of whatever is available.

Frequently asked questions

When is Milan Fashion Week in September 2026?+
The women's fashion week (Spring/Summer 2027 collections) is on the CNMI calendar from September 22 to 28, 2026, with around sixty shows - physical and digital - spread across the city.
How much does private car demand increase during Fashion Week?+
Roughly 10 times that of a normal week (NexaRide internal data), and across every format: single transfers, hourly service and cars at disposal for several days.
What is the most critical time for getting around during Fashion Week?+
The hours before the shows: Fashion Week traffic stacks on top of Milan's normal traffic. Our drivers' rule is to move with a generous time buffer, because the show calendar waits for no one.
Which airports do Fashion Week guests arrive at?+
From all over the world, mostly via Linate and Malpensa, with arrivals concentrated in the days immediately before the calendar opens.
Apps or a chauffeur (NCC) during Fashion Week?+
The most common mistake is expecting app cars to be instantly available: at Fashion Week peak times that is not guaranteed. If you have fixed appointments, securing your own chauffeur in advance is the safer play.
Do taxis cost more during Fashion Week?+
No: meter tariffs are set by the Comune di Milano and do not change during fashion week. The problem is not the price but availability at peak times.

Methodology and sources

This article is part of the NexaRide research series on transport and mobility in Milan.

Internal data: booking requests received (website and WhatsApp) during fashion weeks, compared with weeks without major events; the mix of formats requested; our drivers' operational experience at past editions.

Public sources: the official CNMI (Camera Nazionale della Moda Italiana) calendar for dates and number of shows; taxi tariffs set by the Comune di Milano.
